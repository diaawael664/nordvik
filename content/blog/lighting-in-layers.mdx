A single ceiling light flattens a room. It lights everything evenly, which means nothing stands out and every corner looks a little gray. Good rooms are lit in layers.

## 1. Ambient light

This is the general light that lets you move around: pendants, ceiling lights, or lots of daylight. Put it on a **dimmer**. Full brightness is for cleaning; most evenings need about half.

## 2. Task light

Light for doing things: reading, cooking, working. It should come from the side or behind, never straight down into your eyes.

- Place a floor lamp just behind the shoulder of your reading chair.
- Hang dining pendants 70–80 cm above the table top.
- Choose shades that hide the bulb from where you sit.

## 3. Accent light

Small pools of warm light at different heights make a room feel deeper and calmer: a lamp on a sideboard, a shelf light, a candle.

<Callout title="Warm, not white">
  Use bulbs of 2700 K or lower in living spaces. Cooler light belongs in kitchens and bathrooms.
</Callout>
