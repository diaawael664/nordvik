A sofa is the piece most likely to arrive and not fit. Usually that isn't because the room is too small. It's because something on the way in is. Ten minutes with a tape measure avoids that.

## Start with the room

Mark the sofa's footprint on the floor with masking tape and leave it there for a day. Walk around it, open doors and drawers near it, and sit where you'd sit. You'll know quickly whether it works.

Leave about **45 cm** between the sofa and a coffee table, and **75–90 cm** for main walkways.

## Then measure the way in

Check every doorway, hallway, stairwell and lift between the street and the room. You need four numbers:

| Measurement    | Where to take it                                     |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Door width     | Inside the frame, at the narrowest point             |
| Door height    | From the floor to the underside of the frame         |
| Hallway width  | Narrowest point, including radiators                 |
| Diagonal depth | Back top corner of the sofa to the front bottom edge |

<Callout title="The diagonal trick">
  If the sofa's diagonal depth is smaller than the door width, it can be tilted back and walked through, even if its
  full depth is wider than the door.
</Callout>
