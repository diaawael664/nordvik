The most common rug mistake is buying one that's too small. A rug floating in the middle of the floor makes the furniture around it look disconnected, and the room look smaller.

## Living room

Choose a rug large enough for **at least the front legs** of every seat to sit on it. In most living rooms that means 200 × 300 cm, not 160 × 230 cm.

## Dining room

Add about **60 cm on every side** of the table, so chairs stay on the rug when someone sits down.

| Table                     | Rug                          |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Round, Ø110 cm            | 230 cm round or 200 × 200 cm |
| Rectangular, 180 × 90 cm  | 200 × 300 cm                 |
| Rectangular, 240 × 100 cm | 250 × 350 cm                 |

## Bedroom

Place the rug under the lower two-thirds of the bed so it extends 50–60 cm on each side. Your first step in the morning lands on wool, not cold floor.
